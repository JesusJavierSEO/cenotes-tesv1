What to expect from each destination

Isla Mujeres is the classic escape from Cancun: 20 minutes by catamaran, with Playa Norte regularly ranked among Mexico's best beaches for its shallow turquoise water. Our catamaran tour includes open bar, buffet, snorkeling at the MUSA underwater sculpture museum and parasailing — the most complete way to see the island in a day.

Puerto Morelos and the Cenote Route is the adventurous side of the Riviera: a 22-mile road cutting through jungle with dozens of cenotes along it. Our ATV tours combine dirt-track riding, cenote swimming, zip-lines and a Maya ceremony with tequila tasting.

Tulum pairs a cliffside archaeological site with a bohemian town and the most varied cenotes in the region. Cenotes Casa Tortuga — four packages starting at $45 USD — is our pick for a full cenote day near Tulum.

Want cenotes without the crowds? Three and a half hours from Cancun sits Homun, the town on the cenote ring, where we run our original tours with local guides — a quieter, more authentic experience than the large parks on the Riviera.
